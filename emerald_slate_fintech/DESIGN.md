## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an authoritative, high-clarity digital banking environment tailored for sophisticated consumer and business finance. Rooted in institutional strength yet engineered with contemporary digital agility, it evokes quiet confidence, mathematical precision, and complete fiscal security.

### Design Style & Aesthetic Movement
The design language operates on a **Disciplined Corporate-Modernist** foundation infused with Scandinavian restraint and modern Eastern European fintech ergonomics:
- **Calm Authority:** Surfaces are uncluttered and airy, relying on structural hierarchy rather than decorative friction. Whitespace acts as an intentional structural separator.
- **Architectural Precision:** Every alignment conforms to a disciplined 8px spatial rhythm. Numbers, tables, and balances receive primary optical weight.
- **Tactile Softness:** Sharp corporate stiffness is balanced through generous card radii (12px to 16px), subtle hairline borders, and atmospheric depth, creating a human, premium touch.
- **Data Legibility First:** High-contrast data figures, crisp micro-labels, and standardized tabular layouts reduce cognitive load during complex financial transactions.

## Colors

The palette balances clinical precision with natural stability. A deep institutional emerald leads the system, anchored by heavy graphite neutrals and illuminated by clinical slate tinted whites.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#00A551`):** Active interactive triggers, positive primary CTAs, active states, and system-level focus rings. 
- **Deep Forest (`#0A5E35`):** High-tier banking surfaces, card backgrounds, hover states for primary buttons, and premium indicators.
- **Support Teal (`#0D9488`):** Secondary investment metrics, positive income indicators, and cashflow graph fills.
- **Graphite Neutral (`#111827`):** Primary typographic ink, critical balance metrics, and structural iconography.
- **Muted Slate (`#64748B`):** Metadata, helper text, inactive tabs, and secondary labels.
- **Subtle Hairline Slate (`#E2E8F0`):** Micro-borders, table cell dividers, and container frames.
- **Atmospheric Canvas (`#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9`):** Application backgrounds and inactive card layers.
- **Surface Pure (`#FFFFFF`):** Primary content elevation plane for maximum contrast.

### Functional Accents
- **Income / Positive Delta:** `#059669` (soft forest teal/green)
- **Expense / Negative Delta:** `#E11D48` (controlled rose red; avoids alarmist neon)
- **Alert / Pending:** `#D97706` (warm amber; reserved strictly for confirmations and alerts)

## Typography

Typography pairs geometric distinction for financial scale with unyielding legibility for transaction data.

### Hierarchy & Script Mechanics
- **Display & Financial Metrics (`Manrope`):** Full Cyrillic and Latin support with balanced geometric curves and compact caps. Used for top-level portfolio totals, account values, and section headings. Ensures large monetary digits remain authoritative without feeling playful.
- **Interface & Tabular Data (`Inter`):** Drives all interface components, forms, micro-copy, and continuous reading.
- **Tabular Figures (`tnum`):** All monetary values, transaction listings, and timestamp sequences must strictly enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1` to prevent horizontal jitter across changing balance updates.
- **Label Caps:** Reserved for table headers, ledger categories, and status chips; always rendered uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`+0.05em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial system is based on a strict 8px increment grid (with a 4px half-step for micro-alignment).

### Grid Infrastructure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid locked inside a `1280px` maximum viewport container. Margins: `48px`, Gutters: `24px`. Standard dashboard split allocates 3 columns for side navigation / quick actions, 6 columns for transaction ledgers, and 3 columns for analytics/cards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Margins: `32px`, Gutters: `16px`. Secondary sidebars collapse into drawer panels or swipeable sheet segments.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout. Margins: `16px`, Gutters: `16px`. Cards span full width (4 columns) or pair into 2-column action matrices.

### Spacing Rules
- Dense transactional tables utilize `space-xs` (8px) and `space-sm` (12px) vertical cell padding.
- Card interiors enforce an internal padding of `space-lg` (24px) on desktop, transitioning down to `space-md` (16px) on mobile viewports.
- Spacing between disparate widget modules conforms to `space-xl` (32px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a hybrid strategy: low-contrast boundary lines (`#E2E8F0`) combined with ultra-diffused, cool slate-tinted ambient shadows. This keeps the aesthetic clean and avoids muddy interfaces while remaining distinctly tactile.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`. The foundational canvas behind cards and modules.
- **Tier 1 (Default Surface):** `#FFFFFF`. The primary surface for cards, panels, and modal containers. Outlined with a continuous hairline border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Panels / Dropdowns):** `#FFFFFF`. Box-shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`, Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Overlays / Modals):** `#FFFFFF`. Box-shadow: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 12px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a blurred backdrop: `backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.4)`.

### Specialty Layer: Signature Dark Card
- **Institutional Surface:** `#0A5E35` to `#064E28` linear gradient (135deg). Applied exclusively to primary payment cards and premium status banners. Elevated with ambient emerald under-glow: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(0, 165, 81, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a controlled, ergonomic roundness (`level 2`) to balance financial sobriety with modern digital softness.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Primary Content Cards & Dashboards:** `16px` (`rounded-xl`). Provides distinct separation between balance overviews, transaction statements, and modular cards.
- **Secondary Containers & Inner Groups:** `12px` (`rounded-lg`). Applied to nested groups, inner input zones, and segmented table clusters.
- **Inputs, Buttons & Interactive Elements:** `8px` (`rounded-md`). Enforces reliable, crisp click-targets that contrast naturally against the rounder background surfaces.
- **Status Badges & Avatar Containers:** `9999px` (Full pill). Guarantees instantaneous visual distinction between actionable buttons and passive metadata chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#00A551`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `8px`, height `44px` (desktop) / `48px` (mobile). Hover state shifts to `#008744`. Focus creates a `2px` offset ring in `#00A551`.
- **Secondary (Subtle):** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#111827`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Hover shifts to `#E2E8F0`.
- **Ghost:** Background transparent, text `#0A5E35`, hover shifts to `rgba(0, 165, 81, 0.08)`.

### Inputs & Form Controls
- **Field Box:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, radius `8px`, height `44px`, padding `0 14px`.
- **Typographic Label:** Inter 13px Medium positioned above the container in `#475569`.
- **Active Focus:** Border transitions to `#00A551` with a `3px` aura in `rgba(0, 165, 81, 0.15)`.
- **Amount Inputs:** Prominent `Manrope` font with automatic currency positioning (`₽` / `USD` / `EUR`), formatted dynamically with tabular spacing.

### Cards & Ledger Modules
- Standard card: `#FFFFFF` fill, `16px` border radius, encased by `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- Content structured into distinct rows separated by hairline dividers (`#F1F5F9`).
- Transaction items feature a left-aligned `40px` circular merchant/category icon, 2-line title/timestamp description, and right-aligned monospaced tabular amount. Positive entries prefix with `+` in `#059669`; expenses prefix with `−` in `#111827`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Success/Income:** Background `rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.1)`, text `#059669`, border `1px solid rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.2)`.
- **Neutral/Category:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Alert/Pending:** Background `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.1)`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `18px`, `4px` radius (checkbox) or circular (radio), border `1.5px solid #94A3B8`.
- Checked state: Fill `#00A551` with crisp white SVG check/dot icon.

### Specialized Fintech Elements
- **Account Quick Switcher:** Horizontal sliding pill carousel displaying account mask, currency balance, and miniature trend indicators.
- **Security Keypad / Code Cells:** 6-box input clusters (`48x56px`) with immediate active-state emerald borders and obscured numeric entry.
